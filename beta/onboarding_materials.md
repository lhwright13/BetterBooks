### 2. Installation Guide

#### Step-by-Step TestFlight Installation

**Before You Start**:
- Ensure you have iOS 15+ (iOS 16+ recommended)
- iPhone 8 or newer for best performance
- Stable internet connection
- Apple ID ready

**Installation Steps**:

1. **Accept TestFlight Invitation**
   - Check your email for the TestFlight invite
   - Click "View in TestFlight" or copy the invitation link
   - If TestFlight isn't installed, download it from the App Store

2. **Install BetterBooks**
   - Open TestFlight app
   - Find BetterBooks in your available tests
   - Tap "Install" or "Update"
   - Wait for download to complete

3. **First Launch**
   - Open BetterBooks from your home screen
   - Allow requested permissions (microphone, notifications)
   - Complete the welcome tutorial
   - Choose your first AI persona

4. **Verify Installation**
   - Navigate through main screens
   - Test basic features
   - Check settings for version number
   - If issues, see troubleshooting section below

**Troubleshooting Installation**:
- **Can't find invite**: Check spam/promotions folder
- **TestFlight full**: We'll send a new invite when space opens
- **App won't install**: Restart device and try again
- **Permissions denied**: Go to Settings > Privacy to enable

### 3. First Session Guide

#### Your BetterBooks Journey Starts Here

**Getting Oriented (5 minutes)**
1. **Home Screen**: Browse available books and personas
2. **Library**: Your audiobook collection (limited in beta)
3. **Chat**: Converse with AI about books
4. **Settings**: App preferences and feedback options

**First Interaction (10 minutes)**
1. **Choose a Sample Book**: Start with our demo content
2. **Select a Persona**: Try "English Teacher" for great discussions
3. **Start Listening**: Play a few minutes of audio
4. **Begin Chatting**: Ask "What themes do you notice so far?"
5. **Explore Voice**: Try speaking instead of typing

**Features to Try**:
- **Text Chat**: Type questions about the story
- **Voice Chat**: Speak naturally with the AI
- **Persona Switching**: Try different conversation styles
- **Playback Controls**: Standard audiobook controls
- **Context Awareness**: AI knows where you are in the book

**Sample Conversation Starters**:
- "What do you think about this character?"
- "Can you explain this literary technique?"
- "How does this connect to the themes?"
- "What should I pay attention to next?"

**If Something Doesn't Work**:
- **No response from AI**: Check your internet connection
- **Audio won't play**: Try different headphones/speakers
- **App seems slow**: Close other apps to free memory
- **Microphone not working**: Check iOS privacy settings

### 4. Feature Deep Dive

#### Understanding BetterBooks Features

**AI Personas Explained**
- **English Teacher**: Academic analysis and discussion
- **Language Tutor**: Focus on vocabulary and comprehension
- **Nick Carraway**: Character-based perspective on classics
- **Omniscient Helper**: General book discussion and summaries

**Voice Interaction Tips**
- Speak clearly and at normal pace
- Use natural language (no special commands)
- Wait for visual cue before speaking
- Background noise may affect recognition

**Chat Features**
- Ask follow-up questions to dive deeper
- Request explanations of complex concepts
- Discuss themes, characters, and plot points
- Get recommendations for similar books

**Audio Controls**
- Standard play/pause/skip functionality
- Speed adjustment (0.75x to 2x)
- Chapter navigation
- Bookmark important moments

**Context Awareness**
- AI knows your current position in the book
- Avoids spoilers for content you haven't reached
- References earlier events appropriately
- Maintains conversation continuity

### 5. Beta Testing Guidelines

#### How to Be an Effective Beta Tester

**What We Need from You**
1. **Regular Usage**: Use the app 2-3 times per week minimum
2. **Diverse Testing**: Try different features and scenarios
3. **Detailed Feedback**: Specific examples and context
4. **Bug Reports**: Clear reproduction steps
5. **Feature Ideas**: Suggestions for improvements

**Weekly Testing Routine**
- **Monday**: Check for app updates, try new features
- **Wednesday**: Focus on voice interaction testing
- **Friday**: Complete weekly feedback survey
- **Weekend**: Longer listening sessions

**Quality Feedback Examples**

**Good Bug Report**:
```
"When I asked the English Teacher persona about symbolism 
in Chapter 3 of The Great Gatsby, the app crashed. I was 
using iPhone 13 Pro on iOS 16.4. This happened twice in 
a row when asking the same question."
```

**Poor Bug Report**:
```
"App doesn't work"
```

**Good Feature Request**:
```
"It would be helpful to have a 'bookmark conversation' 
feature so I can save interesting AI discussions and 
refer back to them later while reading."
```

**Testing Scenarios to Focus On**
- First-time user experience
- Long conversation sessions
- Switching between personas mid-book
- Voice input in noisy environments
- App behavior with low battery
- Multitasking with other apps open

### 6. Known Issues & Workarounds

#### Current Beta Limitations

**Backend Connectivity (High Priority)**
- **Issue**: API timeouts causing slow responses
- **Impact**: AI chat may be slow or fail
- **Workaround**: Retry requests, check internet connection
- **Status**: Actively being fixed

**Limited Content Library**
- **Issue**: Few audiobooks available in beta
- **Impact**: Limited testing scenarios
- **Workaround**: Use provided demo content
- **Status**: Adding books weekly

**Voice Recognition Accuracy**
- **Issue**: May not understand accents or background noise
- **Impact**: Voice chat less reliable
- **Workaround**: Use text input as backup
- **Status**: Improving with usage data

**Battery Usage**
- **Issue**: AI processing may drain battery faster
- **Impact**: Shorter listening sessions
- **Workaround**: Keep device plugged in during long sessions
- **Status**: Optimizing in future updates

**Performance on Older Devices**
- **Issue**: Slower response times on iPhone 8/X
- **Impact**: Less smooth experience
- **Workaround**: Close other apps, restart device
- **Status**: Ongoing optimization

### 8. FAQ

#### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I use the app?**
A: Aim for 2-3 sessions per week, 15-30 minutes each. Quality feedback is more valuable than quantity.

**Q: What if I find a critical bug?**
A: Report it immediately via Discord #bug-reports or email beta@betterbooks.app. Include steps to reproduce.

**Q: Can I suggest new AI personas?**
A: Absolutely! Use the feature request form or #feature-requests channel. We love creative ideas.

**Q: Why is the AI sometimes slow to respond?**
A: We're working on backend performance issues. This is our top priority for improvement.

**Q: Can I invite friends to the beta?**
A: Not directly - TestFlight has limited slots. They can apply via our beta application form.

**Q: Will my beta progress carry over to the full release?**
A: We'll do our best to preserve user data, but beta data may need to be reset.

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Updates come through TestFlight automatically. You'll get a notification when available.

**Q: What data does the app collect?**
A: Usage analytics, crash reports, and conversation data for AI improvement. See our privacy policy for details.
